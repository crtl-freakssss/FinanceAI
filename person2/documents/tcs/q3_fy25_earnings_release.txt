TATA CONSULTANCY SERVICES LIMITED (TCS)
PRESS RELEASE & FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS - Q3 FY25
[DEMO / SIMULATED FINANCIAL DOCUMENT FOR HACKATHON]

Page 1: Revenue and Operating Performance
Tata Consultancy Services reported consolidated revenue of INR 60,583 crore for Q3 FY25, reflecting a 4.0% YoY growth in INR terms (1.7% in Constant Currency). Operating Margin (EBIT Margin) expanded by 50 basis points YoY to 25.0%.
Net Income for the quarter was INR 12,434 crore, recording a net margin of 20.5% and YoY growth of 8.2%.

Page 2: Segment & Geographic Breakdown
Growth was led by the Energy, Resources, and Utilities vertical (+11.8% YoY) and Manufacturing (+7.0% YoY). The BFSI (Banking, Financial Services, and Insurance) sector stabilized with sequential revenue expansion of 0.8%.
Geographically, the United Kingdom grew 6.2% YoY, Regional Markets grew 18.5%, while North America showed steady signs of demand recovery in cloud transformation and AI engagements.

Page 3: Total Contract Value (TCV) & Order Book
TCS achieved an order book TCV of $8.1 billion in Q3 FY25. The pipeline remains diversified across enterprise modernization, cloud migration, and generative AI transformation deals. TCS AI WisdomNext platform has been deployed across 300+ enterprise clients.

Page 4: Human Resources & Dividend
Headcount stood at 603,300 employees as of December 31, 2024. LTM IT Services attrition moderated further to 12.5%. The Board of Directors declared an interim dividend of INR 10 per share and a special dividend of INR 18 per equity share.
