Page 1: Financial Performance Highlights
HDFC Bank Limited reported a Net Profit of INR 16,372 crore for the quarter ended December 31, 2024, a growth of 33.5% over the corresponding quarter of the previous year (post-merger baseline). Net Interest Income (NII) grew to INR 28,471 crore from INR 22,987 crore in Q3 FY24.
Net Interest Margin (NIM) on total assets was 3.4%, and 3.6% based on interest-earning assets.

Page 2: Balance Sheet, Deposits, and Advances
Total Balance Sheet size reached INR 36,85,000 crore. Total deposits stood at INR 22,14,000 crore, up 27.7% YoY. CASA deposits grew by 9.5%, with Savings Account deposits at INR 5,79,900 crore and Current Account deposits at INR 2,58,000 crore.
Gross advances grew 62.4% post-merger to INR 24,69,000 crore, led by commercial and rural banking (+17.4%) and retail advances (+11.2%).

Page 3: Asset Quality & Capital Adequacy
Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA) stood at 1.26% of gross advances, compared to 1.34% in the previous quarter. Net NPA was 0.31%. Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) under Basel III was 18.4% (Tier 1 CAR at 16.5%), well above regulatory minimum thresholds.
