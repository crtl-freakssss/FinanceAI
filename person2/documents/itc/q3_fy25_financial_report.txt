ITC LIMITED
REPORT ON FINANCIAL RESULTS - Q3 FY25
[DEMO / SIMULATED FINANCIAL DOCUMENT FOR HACKATHON]

Page 1: Standalone and Consolidated Performance
ITC Limited reported Gross Revenue of INR 18,608 crore for Q3 FY25, up 8.5% YoY. Profit After Tax (PAT) stood at INR 5,400 crore, representing an increase of 10.7% YoY.
EBITDA for the quarter rose to INR 6,240 crore with an operating EBITDA margin of 33.5%.

Page 2: Segment Highlights - FMCG, Cigarettes, Hotels, Agri
- FMCG Others: Revenue reached INR 5,218 crore (+7.6% YoY) led by staples, biscuits, and personal care. Segment EBITDA margins expanded by 100 bps to 11.0%.
- Cigarettes: Revenue was INR 7,548 crore (+3.6% YoY) with stable volumes.
- Hotels: Revenue grew 18.2% YoY to INR 842 crore with RevPAR reaching record levels. Demerger of the Hotels business is proceeding as per NCLT timeline.
- Agri Business: Revenue stood at INR 3,250 crore (+5.5% YoY) driven by value-added agri products and leaf tobacco exports.

Page 3: Dividend Declaration
The Board declared an Interim Dividend of INR 6.25 per ordinary share of INR 1.00 each for the financial year ending March 31, 2025.
