RELIANCE INDUSTRIES LIMITED
FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER ENDED DECEMBER 31, 2024 (Q3 FY25)

Page 1: Corporate Overview & Financial Highlights
Reliance Industries Limited (RIL) delivered a robust consolidated EBITDA of INR 44,678 crore for Q3 FY25, representing a year-on-year growth of 11.2%. Gross revenue for the quarter reached INR 2,48,160 crore ($29.8 billion), driven by strong performance in Digital Services (Jio) and Retail segments.
Consolidated Net Profit for the period stood at INR 19,641 crore, up 10.8% YoY. Capital expenditure for the quarter was INR 30,102 crore, focused on 5G rollout expansion and new energy manufacturing facilities in Jamnagar.

Page 2: Segmental Performance - Jio & Retail
Jio Platforms Limited reported quarterly revenue of INR 32,510 crore, an increase of 12.4% YoY. Net subscriber additions stood at 11.8 million, taking the total subscriber base to 481.8 million. Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) improved to INR 182.4 per month, supported by increased data traffic from 5G adoption.
Reliance Retail Ventures Limited delivered revenue of INR 83,063 crore, up 15.6% YoY. Footfalls across 18,774 retail stores crossed 282 million during the festive season.

Page 3: Oil-to-Chemicals (O2C) and Energy Transition
The Oil to Chemicals (O2C) segment revenue stood at INR 1,41,096 crore with an EBITDA of INR 14,065 crore. Downstream chemical margins remained steady despite global feedstock volatility. Refinery throughput was recorded at 18.7 MMT.
In New Energy, construction of the 20 GW Solar Photovoltaic Giga-factory is progressing on schedule, with module manufacturing expected to commence by mid-2025.

Page 4: Debt Profile and Cash Flow
Net Debt stood at INR 1,19,340 crore as of December 31, 2024, maintaining a healthy Net Debt to EBITDA ratio of 0.67x. Cash and cash equivalents were INR 1,84,520 crore. Operating cash flows remained strong at INR 38,450 crore for the quarter, providing ample headroom for scheduled growth investments.
